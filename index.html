About Sindh

Sindh is a land of ancient civilization, rich traditions, literature and cultural heritage. Its history is connected with one of the world's oldest urban civilizations.


Sindhi culture is famous for its hospitality, Ajrak, Sindhi Topi, traditional music, poetry, language and food.


Sindh Culture is created to share and celebrate the beauty and heritage of Sindh with people around the world.
